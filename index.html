Pollution Health & Safety Guide

Simple ways to stay aware of changing air quality.

🌱

Good Air Quality

Enjoy normal outdoor activities while staying aware of local conditions.

🌤️

Moderate Air Quality

Most people can continue usual activities. Sensitive individuals should pay attention to symptoms.

😷

Poor Air Quality

Consider reducing prolonged outdoor exertion, especially if you are sensitive to pollution.

🏠

Indoor Protection

Keep indoor spaces clean, avoid smoke indoors and use suitable air filtration when available.
